about me

Hello, I am Mahdi Abdurrahman, a junior software and web developer based in the southeastern United States. I build desktop and web applications, responsive websites, and database-driven solutions. I have a bachelor's degree in Criminal Justice, approximately three years towards a bachelor's in Computer Science, and have continued self-directed study in software development and related technologies.

I specialize in building clean, reusable, and professional websites using modern standards and best practices. I focus on semantic HTML, responsive website design, accessibility, and SEO-friendly structure to create websites that are useful, maintainable, and easy to find.

When not writing code, I continue to research, experiment, test, and refine my development process. For, I am not content to rely on past laurels. Also, I have a strong interest in Mathematics and an appreciation for the Arts, but programming and computer science are my greatest passions!
